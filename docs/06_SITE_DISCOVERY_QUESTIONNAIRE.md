# 06 — Site-discovery questionnaire

**Document:** AGR-OPS-060 · **Revision:** A · **Use:** Complete before freezing AP-01 geometry, first cassette priorities, or a pilot quote.

Photograph and measure rather than answering from memory. Record a drawing/photo reference and an owner for each response. “Unknown” is a valid answer; it becomes a risk/action, not an assumption.

| Question | Response / evidence |

## Decision outputs from the survey

The team must produce these controlled outputs within one week of completing the survey:

1. dimensioned site/route/plot drawing and ODD statement;
2. AP-01 fit assessment, including whether AP-02 adjustable field undercarriage is required;
3. prioritized phase-one cassette(s), throughput KPI and human workflow;
4. process/legal owner and approval plan for eggs, feed, chemicals, waste and crop actions;
5. preliminary hazard log and protective-zone/mode concept;
6. power/network/charging/service layout; and
7. a signed list of assumptions, unknowns, decisions and changes to the concept package.
